Blue whale

SpeciesBalaenoptera musculus

The blue whale (Balaenoptera musculus) is a marine mammal and a baleen whale. Reaching a maximum confirmed length of 29.9 meters and weighing up to 199 tonnes, it is the largest animal known ever to have existed.[1]

This article is about the animal.

Description[edit]

The blue whale has a long tapering body that appears stretched in comparison with the stockier look of other whales.
